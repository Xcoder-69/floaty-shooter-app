PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Floaty Shooter ("Game", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you play our game across web browsers (including CrazyGames) or mobile platforms (Android application).

1. DATA WE COLLECT

a) No Personal Data Collected Directly: We do not collect, request, or store personal identifiers such as your real name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, or precise geolocation.

b) Locally Stored Game Data: The Game stores your gameplay progress (unlocked chapters, level stars, coin balance, inventory items, audio toggles, and local high scores) on your device using localStorage (for web browsers) or internal application sandbox storage (for Android). This data stays strictly on your device.

c) Online Leaderboard Callsign & Scores: When submitting a high score to our global leaderboards, your custom pilot callsign (in-game username), high score, and chapter number are transmitted to our secure leaderboard servers to calculate competitive ranks.

2. HOW WE USE DATA

Any data processed is used solely to:

  • Maintain and resume your gameplay progress, coins, and unlocks locally on your device.
  • Display global ranks, competitive standings, and pilot callsigns on online leaderboards.
  • Provide optional rewarded advertisements to unlock in-game credits, revives, or items.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & ADVERTISING

Floaty Shooter integrates trusted third-party SDKs to provide online features and optional rewarded ads:

  • LootLocker: Provides secure cross-platform global leaderboards and player authentication. See LootLocker Privacy Policy.
  • CrazyGames SDK: When playing on CrazyGames, the platform manages game hosting, analytics, and non-personalized advertising under its own policies. See CrazyGames Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: When playing on Android mobile devices, Google AdMob may serve optional rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may process anonymous device advertising identifiers (e.g. Google Advertising ID) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Floaty Shooter is designed as a family-friendly arcade game suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or process personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). Because we do not collect personal identifiers, the Game is inherently compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR Article 8.

5. DATA RETENTION & YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR & CCPA)

a) Data Retention: Local game save data is retained on your device until you clear your browser cache/storage or uninstall the Android application.

b) Right to Erase / Reset: You can delete all your stored gameplay progress at any time by clearing your browser's site data or Android application storage.

c) Leaderboard Data Removal: If you wish to have your public pilot callsign or leaderboard score removed from our global standings, simply contact us with your callsign details.

6. SECURITY

We prioritize player security. Local save states are isolated in your browser or Android secure application sandbox. Leaderboard communication uses industry-standard HTTPS encryption.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect game enhancements, updated legal requirements, or platform modifications. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this document indicates the effective date of the latest version.
