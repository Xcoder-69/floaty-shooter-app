=====================================================
  FLOATY SHOOTER -- ALL FUN LEVELS REFERENCE
=====================================================

Fun levels are special challenge levels that appear at
fixed intervals in each chapter. Completing the objective
earns bonus coins!

RATIO:
  10 Levels  -> 2 Fun Levels
  15 Levels  -> 3 Fun Levels
  20 Levels  -> 4 Fun Levels
  25 Levels  -> 5 Fun Levels
  30 Levels  -> 6 Fun Levels
  40 Levels  -> 8 Fun Levels
  * Fun levels never appear on Level 1 or Boss levels!

MISSION TYPES:
  [skull]  Elimination   -- Kill N enemies
  [timer]  Endurance     -- Survive for N seconds
  [shield] Untouchable   -- Take no damage for N seconds
  [clock]  Time Attack   -- Kill N enemies in 60 seconds
  [dove]   Pacifist      -- Survive Ns without killing
